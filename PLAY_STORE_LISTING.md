Protect your spine, neck, and posture during long work hours with smart, timed micro-breaks.

  Timers start automatically the moment you open the app. Always know exactly when your next break is coming.

  Never miss a break. Audible ringtones, continuous vibration, and lockscreen wakeups alert you right on schedule with motivational reminders.
• 🧘 Targeted Posture Resets:
  Gentle, safe physical therapy exercises designed specifically for cervical vertebrae (C5–C7) and lumbar discs (L4–S1).
• 🌙 Sleep Window Protection:
  Set your personal wake-up and bedtime hours. Alarms are completely silenced while you rest.

  Zero ads, zero accounts required, and zero personal data collected. All preferences and records stay securely on your phone.

Start caring for your spine today with SpineHealth: Back & Neck!
