# Privacy Policy for SpineHealth: Back & Neck

**Last Updated: November 24, 2026**  
**Developer / Publisher: trialrun**

At **trialrun**, we prioritize your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how **SpineHealth: Back & Neck** ("the App", "we", "our") handles user information and device permissions.

---

### 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
- **We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.**
- The App does not require user accounts, email registration, phone numbers, or cloud profiles.
- All user preferences (such as your disc condition, custom wake-up time, bedtime, and exercise completion history) are stored **locally and exclusively on your device** using Android's private local storage (Room database and SharedPreferences).

---

### 2. Device Permissions and Why They Are Needed
The App utilizes only essential Android system permissions strictly required to provide timely micro-break alarms and posture alerts:

* **Exact Alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` / `USE_EXACT_ALARM`)**:
  Required to deliver reliable, millisecond-accurate micro-break notifications and decompression reminders even when the device enters low-power Doze mode.
* **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)**:
  Required on Android 13+ to display daily break timers, reminder notifications, and streak notifications.
* **Full-Screen Alerts (`USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT`)**:
  Enables the App to show the full-screen alarm popup (with audio and vibration) when your screen is locked, prompting you to decompress and stretch on time.
* **Foreground Service (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE`)**:
  Powers the reliable background timing engine to ensure your daily work/rest schedule runs seamlessly throughout your active hours.
* **Vibration (`VIBRATE`)**:
  Provides repeating haptic feedback when a break alarm rings so you don't miss scheduled micro-breaks.
* **Internet Permission (`INTERNET`)**:
  Used only for loading optional exercise demonstration reference videos if applicable. No personal information or analytics are transmitted over the network.
