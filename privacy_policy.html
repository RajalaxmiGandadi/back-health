Privacy Policy

App: SpineHealth: Back & Neck | Developer: trialrun | Effective Date: November 24, 2026

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

At trialrun, we prioritize your personal privacy. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data or health information.

The App does not require account registration, login credentials, emails, or phone numbers. All personal preferences (condition selection, customized sleep and wake schedules, and activity history) are stored exclusively and locally on your physical device.

2. Device Permissions and Why They Are Needed

The App requests standard Android device permissions strictly required to provide timely micro-break alarms and posture alerts:

  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM: Delivers accurate micro-break notifications and decompression alerts right on schedule.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Displays daily break reminders, timer status, and routine notifications.
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT: Presents the alarm popup with sound and vibration over the lock screen so you never miss a scheduled decompression break.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Keeps the micro-break timing cycle engine active on your device during your waking hours.
  • VIBRATE: Provides repeating haptic feedback when a break alarm fires.
  • INTERNET: Used only for optional exercise guide videos. No personal information is transmitted.

3. Third-Party Services & Tracking

The App does not include advertising networks, behavioral tracking tools, or third-party analytics SDKs.
